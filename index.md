# PIPLNCH Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

PIPLNCH ("the app") is an Android home-screen launcher. This page explains what the app does with information on your phone.

**In short: the app has no account, no ads and no analytics. The things it reads from your phone stay on your phone. It does not sell or share your personal information.**

## What the app uses, and why

The app asks for access only to the parts of your phone it shows on screen. You can refuse any of them and the rest of the app keeps working.

| Access | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Installed apps | To show and open your apps in the app drawer. |
| Notifications (notification access) | To show your notifications on the Status screen and let you clear, snooze or reply to them. |
| Contacts | To show your contacts in the Crew tab. |
| Calendar | To show your upcoming events as quests. |
| Location | To show your position on the map. |
| Music and audio files | To list and play the songs on your phone. |
| All files access | To browse, preview, zip, move and delete files in the Files tab, and to read lyrics files next to your songs. |
| Alarms, vibration, notifications | To ring alarms and timers, and to give touch feedback. |
| Change system settings, Bluetooth, Do not disturb | Only for the quick-setting buttons (for example brightness or silent mode) when you press them. |

## Where your information goes

- **On your phone only:** contacts, calendar, files, notes, tasks, settings and favourites are read and shown on your phone. They are not uploaded anywhere by the app.
- **Calls and texts** are never read or sent by the app. Tapping CALL or TEXT opens your phone's own dialler or messages app. The app only keeps a small count on your phone of how many times you tapped them for each contact (for the "in touch" bars).
- **One-time codes** in your notifications are shown on screen while the notification is active. They are not saved.
- **Deleted files** are moved to a hidden bin folder on your phone storage for 30 days before they are removed for good.

## Internet services the app talks to

Some features need the internet. These services receive only what is needed for the feature:

- **Radio:** the app asks the free radio-browser.info directory for station lists and tells it when you play a station (a station id, to help popularity counts). The audio comes straight from each station's own server, which can see your IP address, as with any web radio.
- **Weather:** the city name you type is sent to Open-Meteo to find it and to get the forecast.
- **Map:** the map downloads map tiles from OpenStreetMap-based servers. The tile requests show roughly which area you are looking at.
- **Station logos and radio song titles** are fetched from the station's own web address.

These services have their own privacy policies. The app does not send them your name, contacts, messages or files.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create accounts or ask for your name or email.
- It does not show ads or use advertising IDs.
- It does not include analytics or tracking tools.
- It does not sell or share personal information.
- It does not record audio or use the camera.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices

You can turn any permission off at any time in Android Settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes everything the app saved on your phone (settings, notes, favourites and its call and text counts).
